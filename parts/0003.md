# FEN Parsing
[FEN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forsyth%E2%80%93Edwards_Notation) (Forsyth-Edwards Notation) is a standard notation for describing a particular board position of a chess game. The purpose of FEN is to provide all the necessary information to restart a game from a particular position.

---

## Reading
`Position::from_fen` parses the FEN string part by part - piece placement, side to move, castling rights, en passant square, halfmove clock, and fullmove number. `"startpos"` is accepted to mean the starting position.

---

## Writing
`Position::get_fen` creates a FEN string from the a position.
